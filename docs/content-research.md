# Content and policy research notes

Research checked on 27 November 2026. This is implementation research, not legal advice or a legal opinion.

## Indian supplement and claims references

- [FSSAI health supplements and nutraceuticals standards](https://fssai.gov.in/standards/health-supplements)
- [FSSAI Advertising and Claims Regulations compendium](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Compendium_Advertising_Claims_Regulations_14_12_2022.pdf)

Ingredient descriptions on the site are limited to the supplied CIRCADEN product brief. That brief itself says final product claims must be checked against the finished formulation, substantiation, product label, and applicable FSSAI rules. No clinical trial, test result, certification, or finished batch COA was supplied. Accordingly, quality pages disclose missing documents rather than claiming tests are complete. Product safety, amounts, directions, warnings, and final claims must be copied from the approved label before sale.

## India consumer e-commerce disclosures

- [Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 — Department of Consumer Affairs](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/E%20commerce%20rules.pdf)

The current site is a static preview. It has no checkout, payment, dispatch, contact form, or grievance channel. Its legal pages explicitly identify the missing seller identity, address, support details, shipping, and refund facts. These are not invented, and the preview policies must not be represented as complete live-sale terms. Before accepting orders, the seller should verify the disclosures, prices, delivery and refund process, and working customer-care and grievance details required for the actual business model.

## Indian personal data protection

- [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/22037?view_type=browse)
- [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 — MeitY](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa)
- [Commencement notification, 13 November 2025 — MeitY](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf)

The Privacy page describes the implementation as a preview, records that the bag state is stored in local storage, discloses the Google Fonts request and unknown hosting-log retention, and avoids promising statutory timelines beyond what is in force. The production privacy notice should be rechecked against commencement dates, actual processors, and final hosting configuration at launch.
